# Hairwitness Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 24, 2026**

[English](/hairwitness-legal/privacy/en/) · [Русский](/hairwitness-legal/privacy/ru/) · [Español](/hairwitness-legal/privacy/es/) · [Français](/hairwitness-legal/privacy/fr/)

## In short

Hairwitness (“the app”) is a private hair photo diary. Your photos and notes stay on your device. The app has no account, no developer servers, no advertising, no analytics and no third‑party trackers. We do not collect, sell, rent or share your personal data.

## What the app stores

When you use the app you may take or import photos of your hair and scalp and enter care items, procedure records, life events, notes, blind check answers, weekly shedding counts and settings. This information is stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage. The developer cannot see it or access it.

## Permissions and optional features

- **Camera.** Used only to take your progress photos inside the app.
- **Importing photos.** When you import older photos, you pick them in the system photo picker. The app receives only the photos you choose and has no access to the rest of your library.
- **Saving to Photos.** If you save a comparison card, a report or a video, the app asks only for permission to add it to your library.
- **Face ID and Touch ID.** Biometric checks are handled by iOS. The app never receives your biometric data.
- **Notifications.** Photo‑day reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is used.
- **Widgets, Siri, Shortcuts and the Lock Screen timer.** They run on your device and use data shared only between the app and its widget on the same device.
- **Guest mode.** Lets someone you trust look at photo pairs on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Exports and backup.** Comparison cards, period summaries (image or PDF), the witness video and the backup file are created on your device only when you ask for them. You decide where to send or save them, for example with the iOS share sheet. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. Once a file leaves the app, it is under your control.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Hairwitness Pro subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple (App Store). We do not receive your name, Apple Account, payment card or other payment details. Apple may provide us with aggregated statistics that do not identify you.

## Device backups

If iCloud Backup or backups to a computer are turned on in iOS, your device backup may include the app’s data. Such backups are handled by Apple under your settings and Apple’s privacy policy, and the developer has no access to them.

## What we do not do

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use third‑party SDKs for analytics or advertising. Links to our other apps simply open the App Store.

## Children

Hairwitness is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them. Because the app does not collect data, there is nothing to collect from anyone.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase everything in the app under Settings → Delete everything. Deleting the app also removes its local data.

## Security

Data is stored in your device’s protected storage and benefits from iOS device encryption. You can lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Please keep your device and Apple Account secure.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control your data directly in the app and in your Apple account. If you have a question about this policy, contact us using the link below.

## Medical disclaimer

Hairwitness is a photo diary. It does not diagnose, treat or measure anything, it is not a medical device, and it does not replace advice from a doctor or dermatologist.

## Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the date above. The latest version is always available at this address.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at <https://github.com/Alberto22m/hairwitness-legal/issues>. Please do not post photos or personal or health information in a public issue.
