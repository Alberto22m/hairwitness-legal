# Hairwitness Support

[English](/hairwitness-legal/support/en/) · [Русский](/hairwitness-legal/support/ru/) · [Español](/hairwitness-legal/support/es/) · [Français](/hairwitness-legal/support/fr/)

Need help with Hairwitness? Open an issue at <https://github.com/Alberto22m/hairwitness-legal/issues>. Please mention your device (iPhone or iPad), your iOS version and what happened. Do not post photos or personal or health information in a public issue.

## Subscription

To get Hairwitness Pro back on a new device, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. To cancel, go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or tap Manage subscription in the app’s Settings. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple.

## Moving to a new device

Your photos and diary are stored only on this device. Before switching, open Settings → Create a backup file and save the file somewhere you trust (the file is not encrypted). On the new device, restore it in Settings.

## Photos that do not line up

Shoot in the same room, under the same lamp, with dry or damp hair as before. The previous photo is laid over the camera: match its outline, and the app takes the shot when the angle fits.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete everything removes all photos and records. This cannot be undone. Deleting the app also removes its data.
